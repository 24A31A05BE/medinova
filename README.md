## MediNova – AI-Powered Home Healthcare

MediNova is an AI-powered home healthcare platform designed to provide convenient, affordable, and personalized healthcare services to elderly people, chronically ill patients, mobility-limited patients, and family caregivers.

The platform helps patients access healthcare services from the comfort of their homes. It provides **doctor consultations, home nurse booking, medicine reminders, AI health monitoring, appointment scheduling, digital prescriptions, emergency support, and caregiver updates** through one simple platform.

### Main Benefits

MediNova reduces the need for frequent hospital visits and helps patients save travel time and reduce waiting. It also helps family members stay informed about the patient's healthcare and supports better management of medicines and appointments.

### How It Works

First, the user registers on the MediNova platform. The user then selects the required healthcare service and connects with the appropriate healthcare professional. Finally, the patient receives healthcare support at home.

### Technologies Used

The MediNova website is developed using **HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript** with a responsive design that works on desktops, laptops, tablets, and mobile devices.

### Future Scope

MediNova can be expanded with AI-based health recommendations, online payments, live video consultations, wearable-device integration, medicine delivery, multilingual support, advanced health analytics, and partnerships with hospitals and pharmacies.
